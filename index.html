Card tips paid to your team every Monday, under the rule you set.

For restaurant owners in Dublin who want their staff to trust the tip split.

  1. Every card tip reached the staff pool. In our 12-week pilot with one Dublin restaurant, card tips of €31,754.13 went into a staff pool of €31,754.13, every week.1
  2. Paid out every Monday, by the owner's rule. 12 weekly payouts to 13 staff in the pilot, split by points per hour for each role.2
  3. Staff can see how their pay is worked out, and ask about it privately. Each person sees their own tips, their hours, and team averages, in English or Portuguese, on a phone.3
